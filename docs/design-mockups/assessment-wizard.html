Choose assessment type
Pick which kind of assessment you're creating. This determines how questions are presented to students.

EXAM

Vertical arithmetic written on screen. Students see the full equation, work it out at their own pace, then pick A/B/C/D.

  • Best for formal assessments
  • Students control their pacing
  • Ideal for 30–60 minute sessions

TEST

Flash Anzan speed drill. Numbers flash rapidly one at a time; students compute the running total mentally, then pick the final answer.

  • Builds mental math speed
  • Configurable flash speed
  • Short 5–15 min sessions
